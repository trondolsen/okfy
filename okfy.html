Enable the assistant

okfy is an agent harness: it gives a language model a set of tools and a short, bounded loop of steps. The model — not you — decides which tools to call, so it can search and read this bundle and change what the viewer shows: the filters, the graph, the preview, highlights and decks. With audio output on, it can also speak its commentary aloud.

While a model is enabled, your prompt and the concepts its tools read are sent to the provider you configure, under your own key. Your provider's costs and quota are yours. Nothing leaves the page until you ask a question or list the provider's own models.

The model's answers are AI-generated and can be wrong, so check them against the concepts they cite, and its tool calls act in your viewer on its own initiative. The bundle's text is treated as untrusted data and cannot give the model instructions.

okfy — a single-file, dependency-free viewer for Open Knowledge Format bundles.

Revision

It renders Markdown concepts, their frontmatter (type, tags, status, description) and reference links as an interactive graph you can filter, pan and zoom.

  • Open a folder, or drag one onto the window. On a web server it auto-loads index.md.
  • Press and hold a node, then drag the card to the right edge to build a deck; add pages from the preview with Add to deck.
  • Desktop: click a node to preview, double-click a directory to enter, scroll to zoom.
  • Touch: tap to preview or enter, drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

This tool was generated with AI assistance; its assistant is an AI that can be wrong. Review it before relying on it.
